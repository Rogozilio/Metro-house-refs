# Metro house refs

Moodboard of the references behind the Metro city catalogue of We Are Destroyers: 82 buildings in 7 districts and the central park, and 230 props, as a pan-and-zoom board (one collage per district; the park has its own) or as a list. Residential buildings show the stylised 3/4 refs sent to Phantasm (November 2026), except the owner's Residential style set of 19 November 2026 (8 buildings, 10 props - the young street tree generated in GPT Image from the set's two trees; a replaced main follows as the last view). The owner took 7 Residential buildings and the billboard off the board the same day (the three panel blocks, corner_deli_row, retro_diner, corner_pharmacy, porch_house), then laundromat and walkup_tenement_row; every other building shows a stylised 3/4 made in GPT Image (17 November 2026), followed by a Blender render of the in-game model and the SlopMachine reference views the model was built from. Every prop opens on a stylised 3/4 made in GPT Image (17 November 2026) from its former main view, which follows it; 58 catalogue props have no reference and were stylised from the render of the game model.

Static site for GitHub Pages: `index.html`; buildings in `img/` (board thumbnails `img/b/`, list thumbnails `img/t/`, views `img/v/<id>/`), props in `img/p/` (`b/`, `t/`, extra refs `v/<id>/`).

After replacing an image in place, run `python tools/stamp_images.py` before committing: it gives every replaced image a `?v=` stamp in `index.html`, because Pages lets browsers keep an image for 10 minutes.
